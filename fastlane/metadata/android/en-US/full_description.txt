Dosya Okuyucu is a fast, clean and 100% ad-free companion for your documents and local storage. Core reading, file management and transfer tools are free with no account required and no tracking.

Included free:

• PDF reading & markup: highlights, bookmarks, interactive form filling, handwritten signature, password-protected PDFs and offline translation
• Document scanner & on-device OCR: scan pages with your camera offline (with downloadable offline Arabic script support alongside Latin)
• Word, Excel & PowerPoint: open and make basic edits without breaking layout, with mobile reading/writing view and draft recovery
• File manager: type filters, per-folder sorting, recycle bin with undo, archive browser, USB OTG (read-only) & SD card, SMB/SFTP/WebDAV, encrypted vault and space cleanup
• Nearby transfer: send files wirelessly to a nearby phone or local browser without uploading anything to a server
• Media & utilities: built-in gallery and player, precise image resizing/crop, QR-code text sharing and paper/dark themes in EN, TR, AR, ID and MS

Optional one-time Pro upgrade (clearly badged `PRO` inside the app):

Unlock all power-user tools with a single one-time in-app purchase (no core subscriptions):

• Full PDF Editor: edit paragraphs in place, merge/split, reorder/rotate pages, headers/footers, digital signatures, searchable OCR and watermarks
• Smart Folders: choose which file types and folders to scan, organize invoices, bank receipts, official records and screenshots locally (500-file starter allowance included; extra cloud quota is optional via subscription or your own Gemini key)
• Audiobook Studio: convert documents into chaptered offline audiobooks
• Local Video Shrink and Pro Word & Slides tools

Permissions: Nothing is requested at launch. Opening incoming documents needs no storage permission; "All files access" is asked only if you choose to browse device folders in the file manager.
