One fast, ad-free app for every file on your phone — PDF reading and in-place paragraph editing with OCR and password support; Word, Excel and PowerPoint editing that keeps formatting (mobile writing view, formulas, in-place slide text, autosave and draft recovery); Nearby wireless sending to a nearby phone or a browser on the same network — no shared Wi-Fi needed, nothing uploaded, resumes where it left off; audiobooks from your documents with natural on-device Turkish AI voices; a full file manager with USB/SD support and a secure vault; a job result screen that shows what happened to each file, with undo; a library shelf for PDFs and e-books; smart cleanup that finds greeting pictures, memes and blurry shots in WhatsApp; QR-code note sharing; precise image resizing; gentle off-white and dark themes. Your files stay on your device — no ads, no tracking.
