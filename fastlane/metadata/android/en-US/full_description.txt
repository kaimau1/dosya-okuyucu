Dosya Okuyucu is a fast, clean and 100% ad-free companion for your documents and local storage. Core reading, file management and transfer tools are free with no account required and no tracking.

Included free in Lite:

• PDF reading & markup: highlights, bookmarks, interactive form filling, handwritten signature, password-protected PDFs and offline translation
• Document scanner & on-device OCR: scan pages with your camera offline
• Word, Excel & PowerPoint: open and make basic edits without breaking layout, with mobile reading/writing view and draft recovery
• File manager: type filters, per-folder sorting, recycle bin with undo, archive browser, USB OTG (read-only) & SD card, SMB/SFTP/WebDAV and encrypted vault
• Nearby transfer: send files wirelessly to a nearby phone or local browser without uploading anything to a server
• Media & utilities: built-in gallery and player, precise image resizing/crop, QR-code text sharing and gentle paper/dark themes in EN, TR and AR

Separated in the paid Pro app (clearly badged `PRO` inside Lite):

In-place PDF paragraph and page editing, Smart Folders (offline rules + optional BYOK Gemini), on-device Audiobook studio and video compression.

Permissions: Nothing is requested at launch. Opening incoming documents needs no storage permission; "All files access" is asked only if you choose to browse device folders in the file manager.
